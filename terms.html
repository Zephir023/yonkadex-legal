YonKaDex - Terms of Service

Last updated: November 6, 2026

1. About YonKaDex

YonKaDex is an experimental Discord bot inspired by collectible character games. It is an independent project and is not officially affiliated with YonKaGor or Discord.

2. Experimental Service

YonKaDex is provided for testing and entertainment. Features may change, become unavailable, or be reset during development. No permanent availability or preservation of collected items is guaranteed.

3. Acceptable Use

Users must not exploit bugs, interfere with the bot, abuse its commands, or attempt to access systems or data without permission.

4. Virtual Collectibles

Collectibles are virtual items within the bot. They have no real-world monetary value and do not represent ownership of the original characters or artwork.

5. Availability and Changes

The project may be updated, modified, suspended, or discontinued at any time during its experimental phase.

6. Contact

For questions or support, contact the project administrators through the YonKaDex testing server.
