YonKaDex - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

1. Introduction

YonKaDex is an independent, experimental Discord bot based on the open-source BallsDex project. This policy explains how information may be processed when using YonKaDex.

2. Information Processed

Depending on enabled features, YonKaDex may process:

  • Discord user IDs, to identify players and their collections.
  • Discord server IDs, to manage server-specific settings.
  • Discord channel IDs, to determine where collectibles can appear.
  • Collectible ownership and collection information.
  • Trade history and player or server preferences.

3. Message Content

The underlying BallsDex software may process message content for gameplay and anti-cheat functionality. According to the upstream project's policy, message content is not retained as a message history. YonKaDex's implementation will be reviewed to verify this behavior.

4. Storage and Access

Persistent data is stored in the database configured by the YonKaDex operator. Access is restricted to authorized project administrators. The exact hosting and retention arrangements depend on the deployment.

5. Sharing

YonKaDex does not intend to sell personal data. Information may be processed by Discord and infrastructure providers involved in operating the service.

6. Data Requests

Users may contact the YonKaDex project administrator through the official testing server to ask about their stored information or request its deletion.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated as YonKaDex develops. Significant changes will be reflected on this page.

8. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the YonKaDex project administrator through the official testing server.
